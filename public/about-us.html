Welcome to Zafran

The Sroa Family, the visionary founders of Zafran, have been dedicated to bringing the rich and diverse flavors of Indian cuisine to Delhi. With a passion for authentic culinary experiences, they have created a welcoming atmosphere where guests can enjoy traditional Indian dishes made with the freshest ingredients. Their commitment to quality and hospitality has made Zafran a beloved dining destination in Delhi community.

Meet the Family

Ramesh Kumar Sharma was born in Old Delhi and grew up in the bustling spice markets of Khari Baoli. Ramesh and his wife Sunita created Zafran in 1998. Ramesh has been a resident of Delhi all his life and is blessed to run and operate the best Indian restaurant in the capital.
Rahul “Raj” Sharma is the son of Ramesh Sharma and has been working for Zafran since he was in school. He went to the Institute of Hotel Management (IHM) Pusa for culinary arts & restaurant management.
Rahul got married to Priya Sharma in 2016, and since then, Rahul & Priya have taken Zafran to the next level.
In 2020, Raj & Priya purchased an old heritage haveli in Connaught Place with an idea to expand new horizons & create a restaurant to their liking, with an attached spice bazaar & valet parking.

Community Support

Zafran is well respected for our commitment to our community in Delhi NCR.
Each year since 2004, Zafran has been giving back to the community by hosting an annual free Diwali Langar the night before Diwali, where anyone & everyone is welcome.
We donate on a regular basis to local orphanages, Goonj, Robin Hood Army, & the less fortunate.
